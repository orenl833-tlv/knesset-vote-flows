ADJUSTMENT (raking): the weighted survey row shares S are turned into a joint table diag(A)·S. IPF (500 iterations) then fits it to the EXACT official national totals of both elections. Those totals are summed from the per-booth CEC files per group, with ABSTAIN = registered (bzb) − voters. The later election's totals are rescaled to the earlier electorate size, which spreads register churn proportionally. Raking keeps the survey's odds ratios (who swaps with whom) and fixes its level biases (turnout, recall).
- Cells the survey has at 0 stay about 0: they are floored at 1e-6 before IPF, so raking cannot create a flow nobody reported.
- A group row with no respondents defaults to "stay".
- n_resp_row gives the respondents behind each row. Rows under about 40 (YB, OTHER, ARAB in some waves, SHAS 2019) are very noisy.

COLUMNS: raked = survey after IPF (our best estimate); survey = raw weighted; booth_cls = OUR ecological estimate (simplex cls, venue units, ONE national fit, 10 groups); voters_raked = earlier-election group electorate × raked share. Survey 90% intervals (500-resample bootstrap) exist only for 2021→2022: ines_validation_24_25.csv, message 4.
