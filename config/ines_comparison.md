# INES survey figures used in the headline cards

Source: a separate analysis session ("bgm-voting", 25 Oct 2026) that compared booth-based transfer estimates with the
INES election surveys (respondents' recalled previous vote and current vote, adjusted to the official totals).
The survey files themselves are not in this repository; these are the figures it reported.

| Pair | Move | INES (adjusted) | Ours |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apr 2019 → Sep 2019 | April non-voters → Arab lists | 5–7% | 5.8% |
| Sep 2019 → Mar 2020 | left (Labor-Gesher, Democratic Camp) → Kachol Lavan | 26–27% | 30%, 27% |
| Mar 2020 → Mar 2021 | Joint List → did not vote | 33–45% | 34% |
| Mar 2020 → Mar 2021 | Likud → did not vote | 25% (another booth-based estimate: 4%) | 8% |
| Mar 2021 → Nov 2022 | Likud → HaTzionut HaDatit | ~12% | 11% |
| Mar 2021 → Nov 2022 | Likud stayed Likud | 72% | 84% |

Booth-based estimates see net moves well but miss swaps that cancel inside a ballot box, so they overstate loyalty.
